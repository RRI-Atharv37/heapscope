# Code of conduct

We want HeapScope's issues, discussions and pull requests to be useful and respectful.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Stay on topic, and keep bug reports specific.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments and block people who break these rules. To report
a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or the private route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for anything sensitive.

This is a short adaptation in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
